## 1. What the brand is for

ODDCHAPTER tells true stories that turn. The identity is built on the same promise: one familiar
letterform, and then a turn you did not expect. The O closes; the C does not. They meet in a lens.

Every rule below exists to protect that single idea from decoration.

## 2. Colour

| Token | HEX | RGB | Role | Share of a composition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#17191D` | 23 25 29 | ground, type on light | 55–70 % |
| **Ivory** | `#F4F0E8` | 244 240 232 | ground, type on dark | 25–40 % |
| **Signal Orange** | `#F26A3D` | 242 106 61 | the accent — one per composition | ≤ 8 % |
| **Sage** | `#A7B0A1` | 167 176 161 | support: rules, captions, dividers | ≤ 5 % |

**Contrast (measured, WCAG 2.1):**

| Pair | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Ivory on Ink | 15.48 : 1 | AAA — default pairing |
| Ink on Ivory | 15.48 : 1 | AAA |
| Sage on Ink | 7.85 : 1 | AAA for body text |
| Orange on Ink | 5.79 : 1 | AA — fine for the rule and small accents |
| Orange on Ivory | 2.68 : 1 | **fails** — graphic elements only, never text |

**Rules**

- Orange is a *signal*, not a colour scheme. One orange element per composition: a rule, a chip, a
  single word. If you want a second, delete the first.
- No gradients, no tints, no opacity games on the four tokens. A 40 % Ink is a different colour, and
  it is not ours.
- On photography, Ink and Ivory sit on a scrim — never direct on a busy frame. Orange never sits on
  photography without a solid plate behind it.

## 3. Typography

| | Face | Weights | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | **Space Grotesk** | 700 | headlines, wordmark, numbers, labels |
| Text | **Inter** | 400 / 500 / 600 | body, captions, tagline, UI |

Both are **SIL OFL 1.1**. Download from Google Fonts — no font file is distributed with this
package, by choice (OFL prefers linking to redistribution).

**Setting**

| Style | Face | Size | Tracking | Leading | Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display XL | Space Grotesk 700 | 96–160 px | −0.02 em | 0.95 | sentence |
| Headline | Space Grotesk 700 | 56–72 px | −0.01 em | 1.0 | sentence |
| Thumbnail line | Space Grotesk 700 | 72 / 52 px | 0 | 1.02 | UPPER |
| Body | Inter 400 | 16–18 px | 0 | 1.55 | sentence |
| Caption | Inter 500 | 12–14 px | +0.04 em | 1.4 | sentence |
| Label / eyebrow | Inter 600 | 11–13 px | +0.16 em | 1.2 | UPPER |
| Tagline | Inter 600 | 15–18 px | +0.17 em | 1.2 | UPPER |

Rules: never letterspace lowercase. Never justify. Never fake bold or italic — pick the real
weight. No third typeface, ever, including for a "logo" or a "special episode".

## 4. Layout

- **12-column grid**, 24 px gutter at 1440; 4-column at 390 (mobile).
- Margins: 8 % of the short edge. Nothing crosses the margin except a full-bleed photograph.
- One hairline (1–2 px, Sage or Ivory at 20 %) per panel is allowed. Two is decoration.
- Text aligns to the grid, never centred — except a single display line in a square post.
- Numbers (episode numbers, timestamps) are set in Space Grotesk 700 and may be the one orange element.

---

## 5. Photography and footage

- Real, specific, imperfect. Evidence over beauty: a document, a doorway, a face mid-sentence.
- Available light. No teal-and-orange grade — orange belongs to the identity, not the image.
- Grade toward Ink shadows and Ivory highlights; keep skin honest.
- Do not place the logo on a busy frame. Use the band/panel templates, or a scrim.
- Faces may be cropped; documents may not — if the document is the story, it must be legible.

## 6. Voice, in one page

The narrator is a good editor, not a personality. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No
adjective stacking, no "you won't believe", no three-item lists that exist only for rhythm.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "The ship left with 29 people. It arrived with none." | "A SHOCKING mystery that will leave you speechless!" |
| State the fact, then the turn. | Tease without delivering. |
| Name things precisely. | "Some say…", "it is believed…" |
| Let the silence work. | Tell the viewer how to feel. |

Titles: Title Case or sentence case, never ALL CAPS except thumbnail lines. Indonesian copy uses
the same pacing; the tagline stays English (`KISAH NYATA. DI LUAR DUGAAN.` is the Indonesian
equivalent when a full-Indonesian lockup is required).

## 8. Accessibility

- Body text at Ivory-on-Ink or Ink-on-Ivory only. No grey-on-grey.
- Minimum body size 16 px digital; captions 12 px.
- Never rely on colour alone to carry meaning — pair orange with a label or a shape.
- Subtitles: Inter 500, 42 px at 1080p, max two lines, 90 % opacity Ink plate behind.
- The motion build is decorative; nothing in it carries information that is not also on screen.

---

## 9. The one-sentence version

**Ink ground, Ivory type, one orange signal, Space Grotesk for the headline, Inter for everything
else, and a mark that turns at the junction.**
