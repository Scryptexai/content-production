01 — The mark

An O that closes, a C that doesn't

Same 13-unit stroke, same grid, overlapping by 9 units so the two edges meet in a lens. That lens is the whole idea — it is not a symbol, it is what happens when the two letters are drawn by the same hand.

Below 24 px the junction fills in and the mark reads as a blob. The 32 px favicon carries the mark at 25 px of ink — the absolute floor, and the smallest this identity is ever allowed to appear.

02 — Variants

Four colourways, one geometry

Every logo file is pure paths and rects: no live text, no raster, no embedded fonts. The wordmark is Space Grotesk 700 at +0.02 em, the tagline Inter 600 at +0.17 em, both converted to outlines — so the files open identically on a machine with neither font installed.

05 — Usage

Twelve panels, three of them allowed

Everything on the sheet is rendered, not described. Each rejection states why.

The most common failure is not colour — it is non-uniform scaling. Stretch the mark and the circles become ellipses; the identity is drawn on arcs and arcs do not stretch.

06 — Motion

1.2 seconds, then a hard cut

Arrive, lock, wipe, one orange rule, tagline. No sting, no bounce, no dissolve into the episode.

TimeState
0 msMonogram scales 92 → 100 %, fades in · 180 ms
180 msJunction locks · hold 120 ms
300 msWordmark wipes left to right · 240 ms
540 msOrange rule draws · 180 ms, linear
720 msTagline rises 12 px, fades in · 180 ms
1200 msHold 300 ms, then cut

Orange animates once. The monogram never rotates or draws itself in pieces — the O and the C arrive together because they are one shape.
