# Thumbnail brief — <EPISODE TITLE>

## 1. Choose the system

| Template | Use when the story is… | Layout |
|---|---|---|
| `TEMPLATE_01_PERISTIWA` | an event with a place and a date | full-bleed image + 236 px ink band |
| `TEMPLATE_02_BERTAHAN_HIDUP` | a person enduring something | 500 px ink panel + subject on the right |
| `TEMPLATE_03_SAINS_ANEH` | a strange fact or a body | 452 px top band + two-line title |

## 2. The words

- Maximum **4 words** in the headline. If it needs five, the story is unclear.
- 72 px for the main line, 52 px for a secondary line. Below 52 px it vanishes at 246 px wide.
- One orange element in the whole frame — a word, a chip, or the rule. Not two.

## 3. The image

- One subject, one action, readable at 246 px wide.
- The face or object fills at least a third of the frame; no group shots, no collage.
- Do not put the logo over the subject. It goes in the band or the panel, at monogram size.
- No arrows, circles, emoji, red borders or shocked faces — this is a documentary, not a reaction
  channel.

## 4. The test

Shrink to 246 px wide, put it next to three other thumbnails, look for two seconds.

- Can you read the headline? → if no, cut words.
- Can you tell what the film is about? → if no, change the image, not the words.
- Does it look like the same channel as the last one? → if no, you are off-system.
