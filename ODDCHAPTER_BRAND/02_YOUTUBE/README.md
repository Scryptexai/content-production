## Banner safe area

YouTube crops the 2560 × 1440 banner differently per device. The **all-device safe area is
1546 × 423 px**, centred. The lockup is set at 1080 px — 70 % of the safe width — and sits fully
inside the mobile band, so nothing important is ever cut. `BANNER_CROP_CHECK` shows the TV,
desktop and mobile crops side by side with the dashed safe-area diagram.

## Thumbnail templates

Each template is a **layout with a slot**, not a finished image:

- `id="image-slot"` marks the photo area. It is a clipped group with a diagonal hatch and the
  caption `FOTO · SLOT GAMBAR`. Replace its contents with the episode still; keep the clip.
- Text in the templates is **live text** (editable), unlike the logo files. Sample headlines are
  real Indonesian copy, not lorem ipsum.
- Orange appears once per thumbnail: either the label chip, or one word of the headline.
- Headline size floors: 72 px for the main line, 52 px for a secondary line. Thumbnails are read at
  246 px wide on mobile — anything under 52 px is invisible there.

### Editing a template

1. Open the `.svg` in a vector editor (Illustrator, Affinity, Figma).
2. Replace the contents of the `image-slot` group with the photo, **inside** the existing clip.
3. Retype the headline. Keep the line count; the frame is measured to the current copy.
4. Export PNG at exactly 1280 × 720 (or JPG under 2 MB for upload).

## Palette on thumbnails

Ink ground, Ivory type, one Signal Orange element. Sage only for hairlines and captions. A
thumbnail with two orange elements is off-system.

## End card

`YOUTUBE_END_CARD_1920x1080` is the plate the last 20 seconds cut to. Left third: eyebrow, lockup,
one line of direction. Right third: a 660 × 371 slot for the next episode — either drop a still in,
or leave the slot empty and let YouTube's end-screen editor place its own video element there.

Drawn rules:

- Nothing essential inside the outer 120 px — that band carries YouTube's title bar and controls.
- The bottom bar (monogram + tagline) survives the end-screen overlay at any player width.
- If you use YouTube's editor instead of the slot, remove the slot and its two caption lines from
  the SVG; do not leave an empty frame under a real card.
